Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is Feel Your Best for?
Feel Your Best is for anyone who feels mentally or emotionally off, even if you can't quite name it.
Whether you're navigating anxiety, depression, burnout, grief, ADHD-related overwhelm, relationship stress, or simply feeling disconnected, this space is for you.
You don't need a diagnosis or the perfect words to begin. Just a willingness to take that first step, and our team will walk with you from there.
Can I find a therapist through Feel Your Best?
Absolutely. Feel Your Best connects you with verified, qualified therapists and counsellors for one-on-one online therapy sessions.
All our therapists are trained in evidence-based approaches such as CBT, mindfulness-based therapy, and trauma-informed care. You can begin therapy within minutes of signing up and continue at your own pace, safely and privately, wherever you are in the world.
Does Feel Your Best support severe psychiatric conditions?
Feel Your Best is not a crisis or intensive psychiatric service.
If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts, psychosis, addiction-related issues, or need urgent medical attention, we recommend you reach out to a psychiatrist or emergency service immediately.
We're happy to guide you toward trusted clinical resources, but Feel Your Best is best suited for mild to moderate concerns such as stress, anxiety, depression, burnout, or emotional dysregulation, where therapy and lifestyle care can make the biggest difference.
Can Feel Your Best help if I'm neurodivergent (for example, ADHD, autism, or dyslexia)?
Yes. Many people in our community identify as neurodivergent, and our approach is designed to support different ways of thinking, feeling, and processing the world.
Our therapists and lifestyle experts personalize sessions to your needs, whether that means adjusting pace, communication style, sensory input, or daily structure.
While Feel Your Best doesn't provide diagnostic or medical evaluations, we help with focus, overwhelm, emotional regulation, sensory stress, and burnout, all within a compassionate, judgment-free space.
If you share that you're neurodivergent when you sign up, your Care Specialist will match you with therapists and coaches experienced in this area.
Is Feel Your Best available for NRIs or clients outside India?
Yes, absolutely. Feel Your Best proudly supports NRIs and international clients who want culturally grounded, affordable, and high-quality mental health care.
All therapy and expert sessions are conducted online, and our platform is available across time zones. We match you with Indian-origin or cross-cultural therapists who understand your context, so you can access care that feels familiar, safe, and aligned with your life abroad.
What is included in a Care Plan?
Each Care Plan is a 12-week structured, holistic program designed to strengthen your mental well-being. It includes:
1-on-1 therapy sessions with a qualified therapist or psychologist
Live yoga, mindfulness, and sleep coaching sessions
Personalized nutrition consultations for mood and energy balance
A dedicated Care Specialist who coordinates your plan and checks in regularly
Progress reports that show your emotional and physical growth
All sessions happen online and are scheduled around your life.
How much do your services cost?
At Feel Your Best, we price care with empathy and fairness. Our goal is to make healing sustainable and accessible for everyone, wherever you are in the world.
For clients in India: Pricing starts for as low as ₹700 per session, depending on the expert type, their experience and expertise.
For international clients (NRIs and abroad): Sessions start at $15 USD per session, with flexible payment options and timezone-friendly scheduling.
Integrated Care Plans: Personalized programs that combine therapy, yoga or mindfulness, sleep, and nutrition start around ₹6,500/month (India) or $120/month (International).
We also offer exclusive student discounts all year round, because getting help early shouldn't depend on your wallet.
Your Care Specialist will help you choose the plan that fits your goals and budget best. No pressure, just guidance.

You can make payments securely within our mobile app, using UPI, credit/debit cards, or net banking.

What types of experts will support me?
You'll be supported by a team of compassionate, qualified professionals:
Therapists and counsellors for one-on-one therapy
Yoga, mindfulness, sleep, and nutrition coaches for holistic lifestyle balance
A Care Specialist who coordinates your journey and checks in along the way
Every expert is carefully selected for their credentials, empathy, and ability to meet you exactly where you are.
What's the format and duration of each session?
Therapy: 50 minutes (one-on-one, via video or chat)
Yoga, mindfulness, and sleep coaching: 45 minutes (live group or 1:1)
Nutrition: 45 minutes (personalized consult)
Peer support & check-ins: flexible and ongoing
All sessions happen online, and your Care Specialist helps you schedule everything easily.
Can I change my therapist or lifestyle expert if it doesn't feel right?
Absolutely. If your expert doesn't feel like the right fit, we'll rematch you, no awkward explanations needed.
Just message your Care Specialist, and we'll connect you with another therapist who better suits your preferences and goals. Your comfort and trust come first.
What if I'm unsure about a yoga/mindfulness/sleep coach or nutritionist?
Our lifestyle experts are certified, experienced, and run sessions in small-group or individual formats.
Because of limited availability, 1:1 rematching for lifestyle experts isn't possible at this time, but we take your feedback seriously.
If something doesn't feel right, let us know. Every comment helps us grow and ensures you continue receiving care with kindness and professionalism.
Can I pause or reschedule my sessions?
Yes. We understand that life happens.
You can pause or reschedule your sessions anytime directly in the app. Your Care Specialist will help adjust your plan so you don't lose progress.
Is my information private and secure?
Yes, fully. Your session notes and personal data are encrypted and accessible only to your assigned therapist.
Your Care Specialist and lifestyle experts receive only limited, relevant insights (with your consent) to support coordinated care. We never share or sell data to third parties. Your story is yours, always respected, always protected.
What happens after my Care Plan ends?
You'll receive a full summary of your progress and personalized recommendations for next steps.
After that, you can continue therapy, yoga/mindfulness, sleep, or nutrition sessions individually, or renew your full Care Plan.
Your healing doesn't end when your plan does, and neither does our support.
